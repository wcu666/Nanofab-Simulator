(1) Beam Diameter (\(D\))

考慮空間電荷效應與電壓聚焦：

$$D = 2 \times \left(1.5 + \frac{I}{20} - \frac{V}{100}\right)$$

(2) Sputter Depth (\(h\))

總體積等於速率乘時間，電流 \(I\) 剛好互消，深度僅取決於電壓與面積：

$$h = \frac{Volume}{Area} = \frac{10 \times V}{\pi (D/2)^2}$$

(3) Area Dose

單位面積所承受的總劑量：

$$Area\ Dose = \frac{1000}{\pi (D/2)^2}$$

*\(I\): Current (pA) | \(V\): Voltage (kV)*

4. Project Details & Scientific Background

Fundamental Principles

Breaking the Diffraction Limit

The core of nanofabrication lies in overcoming the Rayleigh criterion:

$$R = 0.61 \frac{\lambda}{NA}$$

Since optical lithography is limited by the wavelength of light, we utilize De Broglie Wavelength of electrons/ions:

$$\lambda = \frac{h}{p}$$

🔬 Space Charge Effect

To achieve sub-10 nm resolution, we operate in the pico-ampere (pA) regime.

Higher beam currents increase the Beam Spot Size due to the Coulomb repulsion between ions (Space Charge Effect), reducing resolution.

Instrument Architecture

This simulation mimics a Dual-Beam FIB-SEM System.
